## Family count and IA policy

`SERVICE_FAMILIES_DISCOVERED=11`. These are documentation groupings, not promises that every related route is public. Each service card must carry one of: **Available**, **Available with paid x402 request**, **Limited**, **Candidate**, or **Pending verification**. A card may link to Test it only when every executed operation has the necessary authority and safety controls.

The top-level product story should use families 1–7, with families 8–11 as evidence/integration/resources. `deploy_change` belongs under preview use cases, not the homepage hero or canonical API Reference.
